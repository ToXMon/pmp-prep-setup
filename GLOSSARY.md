# Glossary

- **Callout:** An instructor statement about study priority, likely exam treatment, or study technique. It records instructor guidance, not PMI's question selection.
- **Verified match:** A reviewed link from a callout to specific course evidence. A callout may have several links with different roles.
- **Drill:** An original practice scenario with a source-supported rubric.
- **Rubric:** The reasoning and evidence a learner uses to assess an answer.
- **Review:** A later attempt at the same concept. Use a different prompt when available.
- **Project Coach:** The Ask PMP mode for applying project management ideas to a specific situation. It distinguishes course-backed guidance from general judgment.
- **PMP Study Coach:** The Ask PMP mode for cited concept explanations and original drills with feedback after the learner answers.
- **Semantic topic:** A concise synthesis of a project management concept that points to course evidence.
- **Course evidence:** A source passage that can support a claim about what the course teaches. A semantic topic helps find that passage.
